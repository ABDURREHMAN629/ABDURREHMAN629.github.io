AR Media Player — Private Android Video Player

A video player I built for my own phone. It plays almost any format through VLC's engine and adds the tools I used to need four different apps for: converting video to MP3, trimming and compressing, zipping files with a password, and saving videos from links. Private videos are encrypted, and the app has no ads, no account and no tracking.

What it does

  • Plays almost everything: MKV, MP4, AVI, MOV, WEBM, HEVC, AV1 and more, with audio and subtitle tracks, speed from 0.25x to 4x, an equalizer, volume boost, sleep timer, background audio and picture-in-picture.
  • Gestures: swipe for brightness, volume and seeking, double-tap to skip, pinch to zoom, and hold to play at 2x. While holding, slide to change the speed, then lock it or lock the screen.
  • Converter: video to MP3 or M4A, trim, and compress or resize with the phone's hardware encoder, falling back to VLC for files the hardware can't read.
  • Files: browse storage, copy, move and delete, and zip or unzip with AES-256 passwords.
  • Downloads: save videos and audio from links in the quality you choose.
  • Library: folders, search, continue watching, history and playlists.
  • Looks: dark, pure black, light or follow-phone themes with eight accent colours, and an animated splash screen.

Privacy by design

  • No ads, analytics, crash reporting or accounts. The internet is used only when downloading.
  • Private videos are encrypted with AES-256-GCM in 1 MB chunks. The key is wrapped by a hardware-backed Android Keystore key, so the files can't be opened outside the app.
  • Optional app lock with PIN or fingerprint. Video names are hidden in notifications and on the lock screen, and the app can be hidden from Recent apps.
  • History, playlists and settings stay on the phone and are left out of backups.
