09 — Questions

Questions clients ask

What kind of ERP software do you build?

Custom ERP and MIS systems built around how your business really works: production, inventory, purchasing, sales, dispatch, payroll and reporting in one place. The system runs in a browser on PCs, tablets and phones, on your own network or in the cloud.

Can you move our data from Microsoft Access or Excel?

Yes. I write migration scripts that carry your old records into the new database, and I keep both systems in step while your team changes over. I have already moved years of factory records from Microsoft Access to MySQL with nothing lost.

Can you add an AI assistant or automation to a system we already have?

Usually yes. I can add an assistant that answers questions from your live data, scheduled reports by email or WhatsApp, automatic backups, and an audit trail of every change. I look at your current system first and tell you honestly what is possible.

How long does a custom ERP take?

It depends on how many departments and screens you need. I deliver in stages, so the first department can start using the system early while the rest is being built.

How much does custom ERP software cost?

The cost depends on the number of modules, users, reports and integrations. Tell me the problem you want solved and I will give you a clear, written quote before any work starts.

Will our data stay private?

Yes. I can build the system to run fully on your own network with no cloud service, so business data never leaves your building. Each user gets their own login and sees only the screens they are given.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I am based in Punjab, Pakistan, and work with clients across Pakistan and remotely worldwide by video call, WhatsApp and email.

Do you give support after the system goes live?

Yes. I train your team, stay available after launch, and keep improving the system as your business changes.
